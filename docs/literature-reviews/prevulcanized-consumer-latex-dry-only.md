# Prevulcanized Consumer Latex: Dry-Only?

> **Safety.** Prevulcanization is a factory liquid-state cure with curatives — not "safer because no oven." Ammonia, accelerators, and unknown bottle chemistry still matter. See [Allergy and Skin Contact](/literature-reviews/allergy-and-skin-contact).

## Executive summary

**Prevulcanized** latex is vulcanized **in the liquid** without waiting for moisture loss. A properly prevulcanized deposit can develop high tensile with **drying alone** — industrial glove lines cite warm-air dry as sufficient. **If overcured** in the tank, rubber is short and weak. Prevulcanized films peak at **lower crosslink** than post-vulcanized latex films — particle integration on drying matters.

**Do not assume every consumer bottle is prevulcanized NR.** Raw concentrate is not a finished article without compounding. Styrene-acrylic theatrical dispersions are a **different polymer family** — name collision is not chemistry. Read [Decoding Consumer Latex Bottle Labels](/literature-reviews/decoding-consumer-latex-bottle-labels) and [Sheet vs Liquid Film Pathways](/literature-reviews/sheet-vs-liquid-film-pathways) before choosing a finish branch.

## Dry-only vs oven-cure {#dry-vs-oven}

| Feedstock | Typical finish branch |
| --- | --- |
| Proper prevulc NR compound | Warm-air **dry** may suffice |
| Raw / under-compounded latex | Post-form **vulcanization** after dry |
| Unknown consumer bottle | Read TDS or treat as non-prevulc until proven |

"Hang to dry" is valid **only** when the liquid was actually prevulcanized NR compound with controlled cure state.

<details>
<summary>What does prevulcanized mean?</summary>

**Prevulcanized** latex has already been partially crosslinked while still liquid — curatives react in the tank before you dip or cast. **Post-vulcanized** film cures after the deposit dries. The two reach different modulus peaks; overcooking prevulc stock in storage weakens the final rubber.

</details>

---

## Shelf life and clarifying {#shelf-life}

Remove unreacted sulfur and zinc oxide by centrifuge or decant to limit further cure in storage. Add antioxidants **after** clarifying so they are not consumed in the heat step.

---

## Do-not-mix {#do-not-mix}

- Prevulc dry-only logic on **raw concentrate**
- Prevulc logic on **bought calendered sheet**
- Medical glove prevulc TDS on **theatrical acrylic bottle**

## Deep dives (optional)

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Prevulcanization tank outline</summary>

Industrial prevulcanization heats compound to 70–80°C, holds, cools, then clarifies. Plant cards — not home tank recipes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Modulus peak comparison</summary>

Prevulcanized films peak at lower relaxed modulus (~0.4–0.5 MPa) than post-vulcanized latex films — integration on drying matters.

</details>
