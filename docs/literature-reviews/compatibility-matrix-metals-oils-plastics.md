# Compatibility Matrix: Metals, Oils, Plastics, and Skin Products

> **Safety.** Solvent cements and some oils are flammable. Oil on latex gloves or sheet can be a barrier *and* a chemical attack vector — read product SDS.

## Executive summary

This is a **never / avoid / conditional / OK** grid for what touches natural-rubber liquid or sheet. **Liquid-latex work:** storage bottles, dip tanks, hoses — brass, copper, and galvanized wet fittings are never acceptable. **Sheet-based work:** vulcanized sheet still suffers copper-catalyzed oxidative aging and oil swell.

Petroleum and mineral-oil skin products attack thin NR barriers. Pair with [Aging, Storage and Care](/literature-reviews/aging-storage-and-care), [Hardware & Metal Contact](/literature-reviews/hardware-metal-contact-snaps-eyelets), and [Home Shop Ventilation](/literature-reviews/home-shop-ventilation-solvent-exposure).

## Matrix — metals {#metals}

| Contact | Liquid latex | Vulcanized sheet | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brass / copper / bronze | **Never** | **Avoid** direct | Catalytic poison; serious deterioration |
| Galvanized (wet) | **Never** | **Avoid** wet | Coagulum formation |
| Black iron / stainless (clean) | **OK** with hygiene | **Conditional** | Plant acceptable; sweat crevice risk |
| Unknown plated snap | N/A | **Avoid** until verified | See hardware article |

## Matrix — oils and plasticizers {#oils}

| Contact | Sheet | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Petroleum / mineral-oil skin product | **Avoid** on wear surface | Glove deterioration lineage |
| Silicone polish / lube | **Conditional** | Release and swell risk — test on scrap |
| NR vs CR vs XNBR oil resistance | CR > NR | Glove film order |

<details>
<summary>What is oil swell?</summary>

**Oil swell** is how much a rubber sample grows when soaked in oil — a lab ranking method (ASTM D471 class), not a garment wear certificate. Chloroprene and nitrile resist oils better than natural rubber.

</details>

## Matrix — skin / body products {#skin-products}

Not medical advice. Regulatory rows prove **petroleum/mineral-oil creams** harm latex glove film. Transfer to fashion sheet under stretch and wear needs your own scrap test. Sunscreen, perfume, and silicone lube: **conditional** until tested.

## Deep dives (optional)

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Copper catalysis and antioxidant reserve</summary>

Trace copper catalyzes unsaturated rubber degradation. For copper exposure, industrial compounding uses **2 phr total antioxidant** — one phr for oxidation, one reserve for copper degradation as film ages.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Ozone and UV storage</summary>

Ozone attacks strained diene rubber. Do not store folded sheet under light at hot ceiling temperatures.

</details>
