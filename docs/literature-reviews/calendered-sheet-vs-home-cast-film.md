# Calendered Sheet vs Home-Cast Film

> **Safety.** Liquid latex work: ammonia, coagulant salts, metal hygiene. See [Home Shop Ventilation](/literature-reviews/home-shop-ventilation-solvent-exposure) and [Allergy and Skin Contact](/literature-reviews/allergy-and-skin-contact).

## Executive summary

**Sheet-based work** buys calendered or catalog sheet (ISO tolerance scope). **Liquid latex work** forms film from liquid via spread, cast, or coagulant dip — handbook physics apply. The same mm label on two products does not imply matched modulus, aging package, or formation quality.

Use this when deciding buy vs make, or when a home-cast panel is compared to vendor sheet on thickness alone. Start with [Sheet vs Liquid Film Pathways](/literature-reviews/sheet-vs-liquid-film-pathways).

## Formation comparison {#formation-comparison}

| | Sheet-based work | Liquid latex work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | Roll goods | Liquid compound |
| Thickness QC | ISO 3302-1 tolerance class | Total solids, dwell, coagulant branch |
| Formation | Mill calendering | Spread, cast, or coagulant dip |

<details>
<summary>What does calendered mean?</summary>

**Calendered** sheet is pressed smooth between heated rollers at the factory to even thickness. You buy it ready to cut and glue — you did not form it from a bottle.

</details>

---

## Sheet-based work {#sheet-based}

Calendered sheet ships under ISO 3302-1 dimensional tolerance classes. Vendor thickness menus (often ~0.25–1 mm) are catalog cues — read the spec sheet for your supplier’s ST class and finish options.

Shop culture on this path usually means [solvent rubber cement](/literature-reviews/adhesives-and-seam-integrity) for seams.

---

## Liquid latex work branches {#liquid-branches}

- **Lab glass spread** (Anode/Teague methods) — handbook lab technique for evaluation films.
- **Belt spreading** — continuous industrial sheet production (patent lineage).
- **Coagulant dip** — economics favor coagulant above ~8 mil dry thickness.
- **Casting** — plaster, slush, rotational, heat-sensitized routes; see [Former Dip vs Mould Cast vs Flat Spread](/literature-reviews/former-dip-vs-mould-cast-vs-flat-spread).
- **Metal ban** — no copper/brass in dip, leach, or storage contact.

---

## Buy vs make {#buy-vs-make}

**Buy** when you want repeatability without dip-plant controls, known modulus from the mill, and immediate cut-and-glue workflow.

**Make** when you need custom compound (color, filler, antidegradant package) — accept formation discipline and property verification on your own samples. Pair with [Gauge, Modulus & Reduction](/literature-reviews/gauge-modulus-reduction-fit-science) and [Pigment & Filler Decks](/literature-reviews/pigment-filler-decks-wearable-nr-film).
