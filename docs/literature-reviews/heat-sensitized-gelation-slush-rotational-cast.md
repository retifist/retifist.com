# Heat-Sensitized Gelation: Slush & Rotational Cast

> **Safety.** Ammonium nitrate sensitizer is added just before use; avoid entraining air. Heat plus closed mould — burn and scald risk. No copper or brass in latex contact.

## Executive summary

**Heat-sensitized** liquid-latex compounds gel when a **heated mould** triggers sensitizer packages (ammonium nitrate, zinc-ammine, polyvinyl methyl ether among industrial examples). Use for **closed-cavity** hollow shells — not former coagulant dips ([Coagulant Dipping](/literature-reviews/coagulant-dipping-wearable-thickness)).

**Slush moulding:** fill mould, dwell, pour out excess — thickness sensitive to compound stability and geometry. **Rotational moulding:** meter compound, multi-axis rotation while heating — more uniform walls. A handbook **Kaysam** cycle cites ~4 min at 82°C gel, cool, leach, supported dry, warm-air cure. **Dry completely before vulcanizing** — moisture causes blisters.

Read this after [Former Dip vs Mould Cast vs Flat Spread](/literature-reviews/former-dip-vs-mould-cast-vs-flat-spread) routes you to a closed metal mould.

---

## Heat-gel vs coagulant-gel {#heat-vs-coagulant}

| Mechanism | Trigger | Typical hardware |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coagulant | Coacervant on **former** | Dip tank, leach |
| Heat-sensitized | **Mould temperature** + sensitizer in compound | Closed metal mould, rotation |

Plaster moulds gel by **water absorption plus calcium diffusion**, not heat sensitizer. Do not mix plaster casting cards with Kaysam rotation cards.

---

## Kaysam industrial cycle {#kaysam-cycle}

Fill closed mould; biaxial rotation; heat to gel (~4 min at 82°C often cited); cool mould; demold wet gel; leach in running water; dry supported at elevated temperature; cure warm-air. Sensitizer (20% ammonium nitrate solution) added **just before use**.

---

## Slush on metal {#slush-metal}

Heat-sensitized natural rubber for slush moulding in metal moulds preheated 90–100°C; gel 2–5 min depending on deposit. Handbook formulations use zinc-ammine or PVME sensitizer packages — industrial examples, not craft recipes.

---

## Rotational casting {#rotational}

Metered compound; rotation while heating. Magnesium moulds are preferred over aluminum for alkaline corrosion resistance in balloon-class processes.

---

## Decision cues {#decision-cues}

```text
Closed hollow cavity + metal mould? → heat-sensitized slush or rotational
Need wall uniformity? → prefer rotational (if equipment exists)
Plaster one-off? → absorption gel branch (former dip article), not metal slush
Have supported dry + leach? → if no, do not scale gel time alone
```
