# Film Defect Atlas: Industrial to Craft

## Executive summary

Liquid-latex casting fails with **repeatable industrial names** before folklore fixes. This article maps craft symptoms to those names and the first check to run — not a photo gallery.

**Pinholes:** entrapped air in compound. **Blown / weak wet gel:** Chloroform Coagulant Number below ~2. **Humidity:** ideal plant **45–50% RH**; extremes break cure or lamination. **Webbing:** fine mist or dewebbing agents. **Late discoloration:** incomplete **leach** leaves coagulant salts that accelerate discoloration under heat and light.

Use alongside [Coagulant Dipping](/literature-reviews/coagulant-dipping-wearable-thickness) and [Former Dip vs Mould Cast vs Flat Spread](/literature-reviews/former-dip-vs-mould-cast-vs-flat-spread). Sheet nicks, glue starve, and seam fish-eyes are **not** this atlas — see [Adhesives and Seam Integrity](/literature-reviews/adhesives-and-seam-integrity).

## Symptom → industrial name → first check {#symptom-table}

| Craft symptom | Industrial term | First check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pinprick leaks / sieve look | Pinholes (air) | Compound air; tank fill method |
| Soft mushy gel, collapses on withdraw | Blown gel / low wet gel | Chloroform No.; coagulant strength |
| Sticky threads in crotch/fingers | Webbing | Dewebbing mist; octyl alcohol level |
| Second coat won't bond | Skin-dry / lamination fail | RH too low; CR needs longer ambient dry |
| Weak cured film, chalky feel | Undercure / moisture | RH too high; oven zones |
| Yellowing/brown after storage | Leach / aging | Warm leach complete? UV packaging? |
| Tank skin or sudden thin deposit | Over/under stability | Mechanical stability window |

---

## Do not confuse with sheet-based work {#sheet-based}

Medical glove hole tests certify dipped gloves, not fashion castings. Pinholes still matter in thin film — but the certification protocol is not your wearable QC checklist.

## Deep dives (optional)

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Functional defect taxonomy</summary>

Industrial dipped-goods QC lists pinholes, weak spots, tears, bead faults, low tensile, dimensional error, and high protein from poor leach. Cause/remedy matrices link insufficient coagulant dry, under-cure, oven temperature, coagulant strength, and total solids — transfer logic carefully to wearable cast panels.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Leach and storage discoloration</summary>

Incomplete leach leaves salts that accelerate discoloration. Roll creases and hot storage compound the effect — see [Aging, Storage and Care](/literature-reviews/aging-storage-and-care).

</details>
