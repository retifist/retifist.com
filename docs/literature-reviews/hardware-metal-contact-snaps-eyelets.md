# Hardware & Metal Contact: Snaps, Eyelets, and Staining Risks

## Executive summary

Snaps and eyelets are stress concentrators **and** metal contact points. Brass, copper, and unknown "antique" platings catalyze NR oxidative aging and leave stain halos that polishing does not fix.

**Liquid-latex makers** should treat vendor finish labels as unverified until base metal is known. **Sheet-based buyers** inspect for green/brown rings and stiffness gradients. Barrier washers reduce stain risk; they do not erase copper chemistry.

No copper, brass, or galvanized in latex or leach-water contact. See [Compatibility Matrix](/literature-reviews/compatibility-matrix-metals-oils-plastics) for the full grid and [Aging, Storage and Care](/literature-reviews/aging-storage-and-care) for oxidative aging context.

---

## Why metals matter {#why-metals}

Trace copper catalyzes degradation of unsaturated rubber. Brass and copper fittings cause **serious deterioration** of rubber in latex. For films exposed to copper, industrial compounding uses **2 phr total antioxidant** — split between oxidation and copper reserve.

---

## Hardware taxonomy {#taxonomy}

| Hardware | Typical contact mode | Primary risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Press snap | Punctured disk stack | Plated brass unknown substrate |
| Eyelet / grommet | Crimped barrel | Crevice + sweat |
| D-ring / buckle | Line load + metal face | Galvanized budget steel |
| Busk steel (corset) | Broad contact | Carbon steel corrosion stain |

No installation steps here — selection and inspection only.

---

## Finish vs substrate {#finish-substrate}

Marketing "gunmetal," "antique brass," or "nickel finish" does not prove substrate. Stainless-looking ≠ stainless. Prefer vendor documentation of base metal; spot-test scrap latex before batch install.

---

## Avoid / conditional / OK {#avoid-ok}

| Metal / finish | Direct NR contact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copper, brass, bronze | **Avoid** | Upgrade material; do not rely on polish |
| Galvanized (especially wet/sweat) | **Avoid** | Coagulum lineage in liquid phase |
| 304/316 stainless (verified) | **Conditional** | Sweat crevice still possible |
| Plastic / nylon washer (inert) | **Conditional** | Barrier — test effectiveness |
| Unknown plated snap | **Avoid** until tested | Batch spot panel |

---

## Inspection cues {#inspection}

- Brown/green halo concentric to fastener
- Stiff ring in sheet around eyelet
- Crack initiation at punched hole under strain
- Accelerated aging vs adjacent panel (compare bend feel)

Replace hardware if halo grows — polishing is cosmetic.
