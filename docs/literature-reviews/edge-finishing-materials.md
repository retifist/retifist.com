# Edge Finishing Materials: Tubes, Tape, and Binding Choices

> **Safety.** Sheet-based edge work often uses solvent rubber cement — fire and fume class. Liquid-latex aqueous systems can still carry ammonia; read SDS.

## Executive summary

Edge finishing is a **material** choice first: NR tube or strip, rubberised textile tape, or industrial spread-coat analog. The wrong family shows up as edge lift, shrink ripple, or a stiff rim that cracks in bend.

**Sheet-based work** usually bonds pre-made NR strip or tube with solvent-cement culture. **Liquid-latex work** can spread aqueous latex on textile tape before combine — with shrink and migration risks. This article names families and risks; it does not teach how to fold a curve.

Aqueous latex adhesives **shrink fabrics**. On porous tape, bond is mainly mechanical if adhesive enters pores; on slick faces, polarity must match. Pair with [Adhesives and Seam Integrity](/literature-reviews/adhesives-and-seam-integrity) for glue family selection and [Latex Textile Bonding](/literature-reviews/latex-textile-bonding) for textile prep.

## Sheet-based vs liquid-latex edge materials {#pathways}

**Sheet-based:** commercial NR strip/tube plus solvent family — water resistance and open time trade-offs vs aqueous systems that shrink fabrics and freeze.

**Liquid-latex:** aqueous spread on textile before combine. Paper saturation shows uncontrolled latex migration causes poor internal strength and **delamination** — analog for heavy wet edge coats.

## Material families {#material-families}

| Family | Typical use | Risk cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NR tube / strip | Smooth finished rim | Gauge match; cement open time |
| Rubberised textile tape | High-stress or curved edge | Prep stack — see textile bonding article |
| Woven binding (cotton) | Absorbent mechanical key | Aqueous shrink |
| Smooth filament tape | Slick face | Polarity bridge required |

## Decision cues {#decision-cues}

```text
Edge geometry?
  straight high-stress → strip/tube family
  tight curve → flexibility + stretch match
Adhesive path?
  sheet-based culture → solvent family tree (adhesives article)
  liquid-latex → shrink check on textile
Surface?
  porous → mechanical key
  slick → polarity match required
```
