# Liquid Latex Ventilation and Ammonia

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/liquid-ventilation-ammonia

> **Safety.** Chemical-hygiene literacy, not a legal compliance certificate. Ammonia and rubber chemicals can irritate or sensitize. If you feel dizzy, nauseated, or get a new rash or breathing symptoms, stop work and get fresh air.

## Executive summary

Liquid latex work means ammonia-preserved latex and dry compounding powders. Prolonged ammonia fumes are a documented hazard; the handbook prefers low-ammonia latex plus ventilation when ammonia is the driver.[^4] The same laboratory health section requires respirators when dumping powders, add acid or alkali to water (never the reverse), wash skin, and acknowledges rubber-chemical sensitization.[^4]

## Ammonia fumes

Workplace ammonia limits (CAS 7664-41-7): OSHA PEL 50 ppm; NIOSH REL 25 ppm / STEL 35 ppm; IDLH 300 ppm.[^22] Open bottles in ventilated space.

Practical Guide: ammonia odor can cause human discomfort, especially above 0.3% ammonia in that composition discussion; not Type I or Type IV.[^11]

---

## Powders and splash

Respirators when dumping powders; wash skin; rubber-chemical sensitization acknowledged.[^4] Weigh ZnO, sulfur, accelerators, and antioxidants with local exhaust or a dust-rated respirator per supplier SDS.

Always add acid or alkali to water, never the reverse. Keep acids and alkalis segregated.[^4]

---

## Metal hygiene

Lab storage: never copper alloys; avoid galvanized fittings that contact latex.[^10] Storage detail: [Liquid latex aging, storage and care](/literature-reviews/liquid-aging-storage-and-care).

---

## Shop rules

1. Read the SDS for the exact latex and each compounding powder.
2. Keep liquid latex off copper, brass, and galvanized contact.[^10]
3. Closed bottles when not pouring.
4. Laundry work clothes that saw powder.[^4]
5. Do not eat in the active work zone.

## Deep dives

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Lab compounding order</summary>

Incoming latex QC: total solids (ASTM D1076), viscosity, pH, mechanical stability, metals, ammonia titration.[^18] Addition order while stirring: stabilizers → ZnO/S/AO/accelerators → fillers/colors → thickeners.[^19] Evaluation films: leach 20–30 min at 40–50 °C then air dry.[^19] Named aging tests are plant QC, not home-shop requirements.

</details>
