# Hardware & Metal Contact: Snaps, Eyelets, and Staining Risks

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/hardware-metal-contact-snaps-eyelets

## Executive summary

Snaps/eyelets = stress + metal contact. Brass/copper catalyze aging and stain.[^1][^2][^3] Verify base metal; inspect halos on worn goods. Barrier washers = risk reduction, not cure.

## Avoid list

| Metal | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Cu/brass/bronze | Avoid direct contact |
| Unknown plate | Test or avoid |
| Verified 304/316 | Conditional |

Liquid phase: no Cu/brass/galvanized in latex or leach water.[^1]

## Inspection cues

Brown/green halo; stiff ring; crack at punched hole; accelerated aging vs adjacent panel.
