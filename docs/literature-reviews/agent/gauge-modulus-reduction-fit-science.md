# Gauge, Modulus & Reduction: Fit Science

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/gauge-modulus-reduction-fit-science

## Executive summary

**Gauge** is thickness. **Modulus** is stiffness at stretch. **Reduction** is pattern geometry. Industrial curves link fillers and modifiers to modulus. Use when thickness and tight-fit lore are conflated.

---

## Definitions

Film must stretch ≥2×.[^1] Gauge (mm), modulus (MPa at extension), reduction (pattern %) are separate levers.

---

## Sheet-based work thickness

ISO 3302-1 tolerance class for calendered sheet.[^2] Vendor menus ~0.25–1 mm.

---

## Liquid latex work modulus levers

Clay filler stiffens.[^3] High-styrene SBR raises modulus and set.[^4] MG graft latex raises modulus/tear.[^5]
