# Film Defect Atlas: Industrial to Craft

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/film-defect-atlas-industrial-to-craft

## Executive summary

Industrial defect names for liquid-latex film: pinholes (air), blown gel (low Chloroform No.), RH windows, webbing/dewebbing, incomplete leach discoloration.[^1][^2][^3]

## Symptom → industrial name → first check

| Craft symptom | Industrial term | First check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pinprick leaks | Pinholes (air) | Compound air; tank fill[^1] |
| Soft mushy gel | Blown gel | Chloroform No.[^1] |
| Sticky threads | Webbing | Dewebbing mist[^1] |
| Second coat won't bond | Lamination fail | RH; CR dry time[^1] |
| Weak cured film | Undercure / moisture | RH; oven[^1] |
| Yellowing after storage | Leach / aging | Warm leach[^2] |

Sheet nicks and seam fish-eyes → article 01, not this atlas.
