# Chlorination for Tack Control & Skin Contact

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/chlorination-tack-control-skin-contact

> **Safety.** Neutralize and rinse; halogenation ≠ allergy cure. See article 03.

## Executive summary

Permanent de-tack: aqueous chlorination in tumbler, ~0.15% Cl, 4.5–7.5 min, then neutralize, rinse, dry 40–46°C.[^1][^2] Sulfur level sets treatment intensity.[^1] Not sheet-based default; not protein removal.

## Solution choice

| Solution | Tack | Discoloration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chlorine water | ≈ bromine | Least |
| Bromine water | Strong | More than Cl |
| Hypochlorite | Less efficient | Similar to Br |

[^1]

## Process discipline

Neutralize 5–7 min; three water rinses; dry 40–46°C. Fresh chlorine per batch.[^2]
