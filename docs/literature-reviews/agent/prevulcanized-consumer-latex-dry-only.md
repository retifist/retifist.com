# Prevulcanized Consumer Latex: Dry-Only?

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/prevulcanized-consumer-latex-dry-only

## Executive summary

True prevulc NR may need only warm-air dry; overcure ruins strength.[^1][^2] Prevulc films peak at lower crosslink than postvulc.[^3] Consumer bottles: verify polymer family — do not assume prevulc NR.

## Dry-only vs oven-cure

| Feedstock | Finish branch |
| --- | --- |
| Proper prevulc NR | Warm-air dry may suffice[^1][^2] |
| Raw / under-compounded | Post-form vulcanization after dry |
| Unknown bottle | Read TDS; treat as non-prevulc until proven |

## Do-not-mix

- Prevulc dry-only on raw concentrate
- Prevulc logic on bought sheet
- Medical glove prevulc TDS on theatrical acrylic bottle
