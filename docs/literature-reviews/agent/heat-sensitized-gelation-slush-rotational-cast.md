# Heat-Sensitized Gelation: Slush & Rotational Cast

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/heat-sensitized-gelation-slush-rotational-cast

## Executive summary

Heat-sensitized compounds gel in **heated closed moulds**. Slush: fill, dwell, pour out.[^1] Rotational: meter + rotate for uniform walls.[^1] Kaysam example: ~4 min @ 82°C gel, leach, supported dry, cure.[^2] Dry before cure prevents blisters.[^3]

## Heat-gel vs coagulant-gel

| Mechanism | Trigger | Hardware |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coagulant | Coacervant on former | Dip tank, leach |
| Heat-sensitized | Mould temperature + sensitizer | Closed metal mould, rotation |

Plaster moulds gel by water absorption + Ca²⁺ diffusion, not heat sensitizer.[^1]

## Decision cues

```text
Closed hollow cavity + metal mould? → heat-sensitized slush or rotational
Need wall uniformity? → prefer rotational (if equipment exists)
Plaster one-off? → absorption gel branch, not metal slush
Have supported dry + leach? → if no, do not scale gel time alone
```
