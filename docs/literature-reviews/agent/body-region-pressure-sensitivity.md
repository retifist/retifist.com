# Body-region pressure sensitivity

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/body-region-pressure-sensitivity

> **Experimental verification in progress.** These planning numbers have not been bench-checked against worn sheet yet. Treat them as working marks, not shop-verified claims.

> **Not medical advice.** Literacy only — not diagnosis or medical compression advice.

## Executive summary

Three maps: Scale **A** acuity/detection (noticeability); Scale **B** PPT (pain ceiling); Scale **C** preferred clothing pressure (garment hoop comfort). Do not average.[^1][^2][^3]

Garment constriction feel: **C** primary, **B** safety on neck/face/chest, **A** for seams/ridges.[^3][^4]

No peer-reviewed fashion NR sheet garment regional clothing-pressure map is in this set. Nearest: textile support-wear / elastic-band studies.[^3][^4]

Fit-planning bridges: [reduction × sensitivity](/literature-reviews/reduction-and-pressure-sensitivity), [pressure targets](/literature-reviews/pattern-reduction-pressure-targets), [Clo3D materials](/literature-reviews/clo3d-material-simulation).

## Constructs

| Scale | Construct | Garment use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Acuity / detection | Noticeability |
| B | PPT | Safety ceiling |
| C | Preferred clothing pressure | Relative hoop budgets |

Stroking pleasantness ≠ detection ranking.[^5] Pain vs touch acuity gradients can reverse on hairy limbs.[^1] Mitsuno clothing-pressure rationale includes autonomic/respiratory concerns at neck/abdomen.[^3][^4]

---

## Scale A

Fingertip/face high acuity; proximal limbs/back coarser.[^1][^6][^7] Density ~241/cm² fingertips vs ~13 arms / ~9 trunk.[^8] Detection: forehead/palm 0.07 g median vs thigh/shin 1 g; arm intermediate.[^5]

---

## Scale B

Nape PPT ~0.55× lumbosacral; shoulder ~0.85× (Kosek sample).[^2] Forehead/sternum lower PPT than many limb joints (Tomschi).[^9] DFNS region/age/sex-specific; women often lower PPT.[^10][^11]

---

## Scale C

Preferred pressure: limbs > neck/abdomen; increases toward extremities; ventral waist low; waistband couples to respiration.[^3][^4][^12] Torso abstract: anterior waist/underbust among the lowest Pa (ordinal only).[^13]

Draft multipliers (arm=1.0) are planning bands — not latex-wear validated.[^14]

---

## Decision cues

- Ridge noticeability → A
- Collar/hood → C + B
- Panel tension budget → C
- Feels-like formula *P*_feels = *P*_target / *m*_C is planning synthesis (sibling review), not a Mitsuno/DFNS result.[^15]
