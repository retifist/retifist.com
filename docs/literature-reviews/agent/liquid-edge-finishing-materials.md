# Liquid Edge Finishing Materials

Human page: https://retifist.com/literature-reviews/liquid-edge-finishing-materials

> **Safety.** Aqueous latex adhesives shrink fabrics and can still carry ammonia.[^1][^2] Product SDS. [Liquid adhesives](/literature-reviews/liquid-adhesives-and-seam-integrity). [Liquid ventilation](/literature-reviews/liquid-ventilation-ammonia).

## Executive summary

Liquid rims are often textile tape or an aqueous spread, then a combine.[^5] Aqueous latex adhesives shrink fabrics.[^1][^2] Porous tape → mechanical key if adhesive enters pores; slick faces → polarity match.[^3][^4] Uncontrolled latex migration (paper-saturation analog) → poor internal strength and delamination.[^6]

## Material families

| Family | Risk cue |
| --- | --- |
| Rubberised textile tape | Prep — [Liquid latex–textile bonding](/literature-reviews/liquid-latex-textile-bonding) |
| Woven cotton binding | Aqueous shrink[^1] |
| Smooth filament tape | Polarity bridge |
| Heavy wet spread | Migration / delamination analog[^6] |

## Decision cues

```text
aqueous → shrink check on textile
porous → mechanical key | slick → polarity match
heavy wet coat → migration / delamination analog
```

Failed rims: [Liquid delamination](/literature-reviews/liquid-delamination-seam-failure). Wet vs dry: [Liquid adhesives](/literature-reviews/liquid-adhesives-and-seam-integrity).

## Deep dives

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Saturation migration</summary>

Control solids and drying rate to avoid migration to the surface, poor internal strength, and delamination. Industrial paper web — transfer carefully to edge coat weight.[^6]

</details>

<details>
<summary>Deep dive: Polarity and porous flow</summary>

Polarity match for non-porous; pore flow plus opposite charge for porous. Higher filler reduces flow into pores.[^3][^4]

</details>
