# Liquid Film Defects

## Executive summary

Liquid-latex deposits fail with repeatable industrial names. This page maps a craft symptom to that name and the first check to run.

**Pinholes:** entrapped air in the compound. **Blown / weak wet gel:** Chloroform Coagulant Number below about 2. **Humidity:** ideal plant **45–50% RH**; extremes break cure or lamination. **Webbing:** fine mist or dewebbing agents. **Late discoloration:** incomplete **leach** leaves coagulant salts that accelerate discoloration under heat and light.

Use alongside [Coagulant dipping](/literature-reviews/liquid-coagulant-dipping-wearable-thickness) and [Former dip, mould cast, and flat spread](/literature-reviews/liquid-former-dip-vs-mould-cast-vs-flat-spread).

## Symptom → industrial name → first check {#symptom-table}

| Craft symptom | Industrial term | First check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pinprick leaks / sieve look | Pinholes (air) | Compound air; tank fill method |
| Soft mushy gel, collapses on withdraw | Blown gel / low wet gel | Chloroform Number; coagulant strength |
| Sticky threads in crotch or fingers | Webbing | Dewebbing mist; octyl alcohol level |
| Second coat will not bond | Skin-dry / lamination fail | RH too low; chloroprene needs longer ambient dry |
| Weak cured film, chalky feel | Undercure / moisture | RH too high; oven zones |
| Yellowing or brown after storage | Leach / aging | Warm leach complete? UV packaging? |
| Tank skin or sudden thin deposit | Over- or under-stability | Mechanical stability window |

## Plant notes that travel {#plant-notes}

Mild vacuum on storage. Do not entrain air when filling a dip tank. Plants treat pinholes as reject.

Webbing remedies in the handbook include a fine mist of octyl alcohol or silicone/oil emulsions. Octyl alcohol in the 0.125–0.25 phr band can reduce oil spotting if you wait about 4 hours.

Functional defects listed industrially also include weak spots, tears, bead faults, low tensile, dimensional error, and high protein from poor leach. Cause/remedy language ties to insufficient coagulant dry, under-cure, oven temperature, coagulant strength, and total solids.
